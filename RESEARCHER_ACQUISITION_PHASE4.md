# Researcher Acquisition & Academic Author Outreach Guide (Phase 4)

**Journal:** Sanmati Spectrum of Knowledge & Emerging Discourse (ISSN 3108-1819)  
**Frequency:** Quarterly (4 Issues per Volume)  
**Model:** Open Access (CC-BY 4.0), Double-Blind Peer Review, Persistent CrossRef DOIs  
**Core Objective:** High-Quality Academic Author Acquisition & Manuscript Ingestion

---

## 1. Ethical & Academic Engagement Principles

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ACADEMIC INTEGRITY & NON-SPAM POLICY**:
> - All outreach must be strictly permission-based, personalized, and relevant to specific academic disciplines.
> - Never scrape generic mailing lists or bulk-blast unsolicited emails.
> - Do NOT claim indexing or metrics that are not independently verifiable (no Scopus/WoS/Impact Factor claims).
> - Emphasize authentic peer review (15–30 days), persistent CrossRef DOIs, open-access reach, and author copyright retention.

## 2. Campaign 1: Call for Papers 2026 (Multidisciplinary)

### 2.1 Target Audiences
1. **University Faculty & Professors:** Assistant Professors, Associate Professors, and Heads of Departments seeking reputable platforms for empirical and theoretical work.
2. **PhD & Doctoral Scholars:** Researchers completing theses or seeking peer-reviewed publication of methodology and findings.
3. **Postgraduate & Master's Researchers:** Advanced students preparing their inaugural academic papers.
4. **Institutional Research Labs & Centers:** Interdisciplinary research groups in science, social sciences, management, and technology.

### 2.2 Core Value Proposition Matrix
| Author Concern | Sanmati Spectrum Advantage |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Review Fairness** | Strict Double-Blind Peer Review (2+ independent external subject experts). |
| **Review Timelines** | Transparent 15–30 day turnaround with point-by-point reviewer feedback. |
| **Permanent Citation** | Every accepted paper is assigned a persistent CrossRef DOI. |
| **Visibility & Impact** | Full Gold Open Access under CC-BY 4.0; structured Google Scholar metadata. |
| **Author Support** | Free downloadable MS Word formatting templates, checklists, and automated pre-checks. |
